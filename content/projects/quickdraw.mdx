## The problem

Claims that a model is fast come from someone else's network and someone else's prompts. The numbers that matter are the ones your own app will hit.

## How it works

Quickdraw records time to first token, tokens per second, latency and cost for your prompts, in CI so the numbers become a trend.

- A command-line tool sends your prompts to a model and times the stream as it arrives.
- Cost comes from a table of public per-token prices.
- Results are written to CSV and JSON so they can be diffed and charted.
- Run on a schedule in CI, each run adds a point to the history instead of being read once and forgotten.

## What it does not do

- It sends one request at a time, so it shows nothing about behaviour under concurrent load.
- Cold and warm starts are not separated, so a slow first call can skew a run.
- Cost uses list prices and ignores any discount you have negotiated.
- The numbers describe your network and prompts, not a general ranking of models.
